=== TELECOM SERVICE REQUESTS - KNOWLEDGE BASE ===

--- New Line Activation ---
Issue: Customer requests activation of a new phone line or data connection.
Resolution Steps:
1. Collect required customer information: ID, address, preferred plan.
2. Run credit check if required by company policy.
3. Select and assign a phone number from the available number pool.
4. Provision the new line in the network management system.
5. Activate SIM card and associate with the new line.
6. Configure voicemail, caller ID, and other default services.
7. Test the new line by making a test call and verifying data connectivity.
8. Send welcome email/SMS with plan details, account information, and support contacts.

--- Service Upgrade/Downgrade ---
Issue: Customer wants to upgrade or downgrade their service tier.
Resolution Steps:
1. Review the customer's current service tier and usage patterns.
2. Present available upgrade/downgrade options with pricing comparison.
3. Check contract terms for any upgrade/downgrade restrictions or fees.
4. Explain prorated charges for mid-cycle changes.
5. Process the service change in the provisioning system.
6. Verify the change takes effect (speed test for internet, channel check for TV).
7. Update billing records to reflect the new service tier.
8. Confirm changes with the customer and provide new service summary.

--- Account Transfer ---
Issue: Customer wants to transfer account ownership to another person.
Resolution Steps:
1. Verify current account holder's identity and authorization.
2. Collect new account holder's personal information and identification.
3. Run credit check on the new account holder if required.
4. Check for any outstanding balance or contract obligations.
5. Process the account transfer in the billing system.
6. Update all contact information, billing address, and payment methods.
7. Generate new account credentials for the new owner.
8. Send confirmation to both old and new account holders.

--- Number Porting ---
Issue: Customer wants to port their number from/to another carrier.
Resolution Steps:
1. Verify the customer has an active account with the current carrier.
2. Obtain the port-out authorization code from the current carrier.
3. Verify number eligibility for porting (check for any blocks or restrictions).
4. Submit the port request through the Number Portability Clearinghouse.
5. Typical porting timeline: 1-3 business days for mobile, 3-10 for landline.
6. Keep the customer informed about the porting status via SMS/email updates.
7. Once ported, verify the number works correctly on the new network.
8. Confirm old account closure and any final billing with the previous carrier.

--- Scheduled Maintenance Notification ---
Issue: Customer inquiring about scheduled maintenance or planned outages.
Resolution Steps:
1. Check the network maintenance calendar for planned activities.
2. Verify if the customer's area is affected by the maintenance window.
3. Provide specific maintenance start time, expected duration, and affected services.
4. Offer alternative connectivity options during the maintenance window if available.
5. Register the customer for automatic status updates during maintenance.
6. If maintenance is extended beyond schedule, proactively notify affected customers.
7. After maintenance, confirm service restoration and request customer verification.
8. Apply service credits if outage exceeds SLA thresholds.
