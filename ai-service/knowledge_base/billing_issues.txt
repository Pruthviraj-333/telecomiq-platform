=== TELECOM BILLING ISSUES - KNOWLEDGE BASE ===

--- Overcharge Investigation ---
Issue: Customer reports charges higher than expected on their monthly bill.
Resolution Steps:
1. Pull up the customer's billing statement for the disputed period.
2. Compare charges against the customer's current plan details and pricing.
3. Check for any overage charges (data, international calls, premium SMS).
4. Verify if any promotional discounts have expired.
5. Check for any add-on services that may have been activated (insurance, cloud storage).
6. Review call/data usage records for any anomalous activity.
7. If overcharge confirmed, initiate credit/adjustment process through billing system.
8. Document the adjustment reason and update customer account notes.

--- Plan Change and Upgrade Requests ---
Issue: Customer wants to change or upgrade their current service plan.
Resolution Steps:
1. Review customer's current plan details and contract terms.
2. Check if the customer is under a contract with early termination fees.
3. Present available plan options that match the customer's usage patterns.
4. Explain any prorated charges for mid-cycle plan changes.
5. Process the plan change in the billing system with effective date.
6. Confirm the new plan details with the customer (price, data limits, features).
7. Send confirmation email/SMS with new plan summary.
8. Set follow-up reminder to check customer satisfaction after first billing cycle.

--- Refund Processing ---
Issue: Customer requests a refund for a billing error or service outage.
Resolution Steps:
1. Verify the refund eligibility based on company refund policy.
2. Calculate the refund amount based on the service disruption period or billing error.
3. Check if a credit note can be applied to the next billing cycle instead.
4. Process the refund through the approved refund workflow.
5. Provide the customer with a refund reference number and expected timeline.
6. For credit card refunds, inform customer it may take 5-10 business days.
7. Document the refund reason in the customer's account history.
8. Escalate to billing supervisor if refund exceeds authorized limit.

--- Invoice Discrepancy ---
Issue: Customer's invoice shows charges that don't match their usage or plan.
Resolution Steps:
1. Download the detailed invoice and itemized call/data records.
2. Cross-reference each line item with the customer's plan allowances.
3. Check for any third-party charges (premium content, app subscriptions).
4. Verify tax calculations are correct for the customer's region.
5. Check for any partial month charges due to service changes.
6. If discrepancy found, generate a corrected invoice.
7. Explain each charge to the customer for transparency.
8. Set up billing alerts if available to prevent future surprises.

--- Autopay Issues ---
Issue: Customer's automatic payment failed or was charged incorrectly.
Resolution Steps:
1. Check the autopay configuration in the billing system.
2. Verify the payment method on file (card expiry, bank account status).
3. Check for any declined transaction records and decline reason codes.
4. If card expired, request updated payment information from customer.
5. Verify the autopay amount matches the expected billing amount.
6. Re-process the payment if it was a temporary bank/card issue.
7. Waive any late payment fees if the failure was on our system's side.
8. Recommend the customer set up backup payment method to avoid future issues.
