=== TELECOM HARDWARE ISSUES - KNOWLEDGE BASE ===

--- Router/Modem Replacement (RMA) ---
Issue: Customer's router or modem is malfunctioning and needs replacement.
Resolution Steps:
1. Confirm the hardware fault through remote diagnostics.
2. Check warranty status and determine if replacement is free or chargeable.
3. Verify the device model and check compatible replacement options.
4. Initiate Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) process.
5. Arrange shipping of replacement device with pre-configured settings.
6. Provide customer with tracking number and expected delivery date.
7. Guide customer through setup of replacement device or schedule technician visit.
8. Arrange collection of defective device within 14 days of replacement delivery.

--- SIM Card Replacement ---
Issue: Customer's SIM card is damaged, lost, or needs upgrade (e.g., to nano-SIM).
Resolution Steps:
1. Verify customer identity through account verification process.
2. If SIM is lost/stolen, immediately deactivate the old SIM to prevent misuse.
3. Determine the required SIM size (standard, micro, nano, or eSIM).
4. Issue new SIM card with the customer's existing phone number.
5. Activate the new SIM and deactivate the old one in the provisioning system.
6. Inform customer about any SIM replacement charges.
7. Test the new SIM by making a call and verifying data connectivity.
8. Update account records with the new SIM (ICCID) number.

--- Set-Top Box Troubleshooting ---
Issue: Customer's TV set-top box is not working properly.
Resolution Steps:
1. Check HDMI/AV cable connections between set-top box and TV.
2. Verify the TV is set to the correct input/source.
3. Power cycle the set-top box: unplug for 30 seconds, then reconnect.
4. Check if the remote control batteries need replacement.
5. Verify the set-top box software is up to date (check for OTA updates).
6. Reset the set-top box to factory defaults if persistent issues.
7. Check the coaxial/ethernet cable connection to the set-top box.
8. If hardware fault confirmed, initiate RMA replacement process.

--- Fiber ONT (Optical Network Terminal) Diagnostics ---
Issue: Fiber ONT device showing error indicators or not functioning.
Resolution Steps:
1. Check all LED indicators: Power (solid green), PON (solid green), LAN (blinking = active).
2. If PON light is off or red, the fiber connection to the central office may be broken.
3. Check fiber cable for any visible damage, especially at entry points.
4. Power cycle the ONT: unplug, wait 60 seconds, reconnect.
5. Check optical power levels through ONT web interface (usually 192.168.1.1).
6. Acceptable RX power range: -8 dBm to -28 dBm. Below -28 dBm indicates a fiber issue.
7. If ONT is unresponsive, try factory reset using the pinhole reset button.
8. Dispatch fiber technician if optical levels are out of range or ONT hardware failure confirmed.

--- Device Compatibility Check ---
Issue: Customer's device is not working properly on the network.
Resolution Steps:
1. Verify device IMEI through the system: dial *#06# on the device.
2. Check if the device supports the required network bands (4G LTE / 5G NR).
3. Verify the device is not blacklisted (IMEI check against GSMA database).
4. Ensure the device is carrier-unlocked or locked to our network.
5. Check APN settings: verify Access Point Name configuration matches network requirements.
6. For VoLTE/VoWiFi, verify device compatibility and enable in device settings.
7. Update device firmware/software to the latest version.
8. If device is incompatible, recommend compatible alternatives from our device catalog.
